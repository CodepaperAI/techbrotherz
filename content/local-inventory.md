# Local content inventory

> Built before any Tier 5 or Tier 6 page, per the Phase 6 brief. This is the
> source for future local pages. **A page with fewer than four genuinely
> distinct, verifiable facts does not ship.**
>
> "Distinct" means true of that page and not of its siblings. The shop's
> address, hours, warranty, prices and walk-in policy are true of every page
> and therefore count for none of them.
>
> Every fact below is sourced. Anything that could not be verified is in
> Section 5 for the client to confirm, not approximated on the page.

## 1. What the shop's address actually is

`3317 17 Ave SE` sits at 33 Street SE on 17 Avenue SE.

Three things follow, and one of them contradicts an assumption in the original brief.

**It is on International Avenue.** The International Avenue Business Revitalization Zone runs along 17 Avenue SE from 26 Street to 61 Street SE, so 3317 is inside it, toward the western end. The BRZ was created in 1993 and covers more than 400 merchants across 35 blocks. [1][2]

**It is not in Forest Lawn.** 17 Avenue SE passes through Inglewood, Albert Park/Radisson Heights, Southview and Forest Lawn, in that order heading east. [3] Forest Lawn proper begins around 36 Street SE. At 33 Street the shop has Albert Park/Radisson Heights to the north and Southview to the south. International Avenue is _centred on_ Forest Lawn, which is why the two get conflated, and a page claiming the shop is in Forest Lawn would be wrong. The Forest Lawn page has to say "a few blocks west of Forest Lawn, on the same street" rather than "in Forest Lawn".

**There is a MAX Purple station at the door.** 33 Street SE Station is on the 17 Avenue Transitway, a dedicated bus-only corridor. MAX Purple, route 307, opened on 19 November 2018 and runs from downtown east along 17 Avenue SE in a median transitway. [4][5] This is the single best local fact the site has, and it belongs on the Calgary pages rather than being spread thin across all of them.

## 4. Recommended cuts

**Cut all three Airdrie pages: `/locations/airdrie`, `/cell-phone-repair-airdrie`, `/laptop-repair-airdrie`.**

Airdrie reaches three verifiable distinct facts, and two of them are reasons not to make the trip. Airdrie is north of Calgary; the shop is in the southeast. There is no shared road, no landmark in common, no direct transit, and no drive time we can state honestly. A page built on that is the stretch the brief warned about, and it would be making a claim to an audience well placed to notice it is thin.

**This does not change `areaServed`.** The business genuinely serves Airdrie customers who choose to travel, and the `LocalBusiness` schema continues to list Calgary, Chestermere and Airdrie on every page. Not publishing a page about a town is not the same as not serving it. Airdrie stays visible through `/locations` and the service-area copy.

**Revised count: 14 new pages rather than 17.** 11 Tier 5, 3 Tier 6.
